    SORA POINTS RULES

    Earn: Points rise with eligible fare and cabin: Economy earns 0.35 points per $1, Premium Economy 0.51, Class Silver 0.75, and Class Gold 1.00. Each completed flight in a cabin adds 5% to that cabin’s next annual earn multiplier, up to +50%; the four cabin classes track separately and reset each calendar year. Sora Plus adds a further 25% bonus. Spend: conversion of 100 points = $1 off an eligible flight, up to its fare. Gold and Platinum can be earned at their lifetime point thresholds or redeemed from your available balance. Redeeming a tier does not increase lifetime points.

    HOW YOUR POINTS GROW

    Your fare sets the base.
    Your loyalty lifts it.

    Each eligible booking earns points in proportion to the ticket price: higher fares earn more. Flights booked during the year build a percentage multiplier, tracked separately for each cabin class. Repeating a cabin’s bookings increases that cabin’s multiplier.

    Economy 0.35 point/$ · +5% yearly flightPremium Economy 0.51 point/$ · +5% yearly flightClass Silver · Business 0.75 point/$ · +5% yearly flightClass Gold · First 1 point/$ · +5% yearly flight
    YOUR EXAMPLE · YEARLY EARNINGS
    5 Premium Economy flightsbooked consistently≈1 First Class flightbooked inconsistently

    The programme is designed for a consistent Premium Economy flyer to earn at a similar yearly pace to an occasional First Class flyer. The fare-linked base amount means final equivalence depends on the published ticket prices.
